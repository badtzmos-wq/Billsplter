## Design Context

### Users
Thai-speaking friends splitting meals and shared expenses on mobile and desktop.
The main task is selecting a shop, recording expenses, and assigning shares quickly.

### Brand Personality
Warm, clear, practical. Keep the existing warm neutrals and blue primary actions.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the established interface. Use compact shop context and make expense entry
the primary visual focus, with shop details revealed on demand.

### Design Principles
- Keep the current shop visible without crowding the expense form.
- Group related fields with readable labels and consistent spacing.
- Keep secondary shop management available through explicit actions.
- Make calculator destinations and applied values clear.
- Support touch controls, keyboard navigation, and narrow screens.
